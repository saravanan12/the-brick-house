The house

A house that breathes.

Completed in 2025 in the Laurie Baker tradition, this home is built of exposed wire-cut brick — no paint or plaster to maintain, and naturally cool rooms through the Tirunelveli summer. Jali screens pull daylight and breeze through every floor, and filler-slab ceilings of clay tile reduce both heat and material.

Two floors hold a 2 BHK home of 1,300 sq ft on a 1,600 sq ft east-facing plot, with Mangalore-tiled verandahs, a covered carport for car and bike, and a rooftop terrace over open paddy fields.

Built responsibly

Natural materials. Smarter living.

Every choice in this house keeps it cooler, lower-maintenance and closer to nature.

Exposed brick

Wire-cut brick walls with no paint or plaster to maintain — naturally cool all year.

Jali ventilation

Perforated brick screens let in daylight and cross-breeze, but keep the heat out.

Filler-slab ceilings

Clay tiles set into the slab — a Baker signature that keeps rooms cooler with less concrete.

Mangalore tiles

Clay-tiled verandah roofs shade the ground floor and shed the monsoon rain.

Rooftop terrace

Brick-paved terrace with open views over the green paddy fields behind the house.

Covered parking

Tiled carport for a car and a bike, behind laser-cut designer metal gates.
